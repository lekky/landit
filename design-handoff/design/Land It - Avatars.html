Avatar set

Thirty-six built-in pictures a rider picks in place of their initial. Inked side profiles on flat pastel circles, split into lids, bare heads and kit, so a rider can find something close to how they actually turn up at the park. Or opt out of a face entirely.

Rules for adding more
Side profile, always

Everyone faces the same way. It keeps a crew list looking like a set rather than a scrapbook.

One pastel behind

Six background tints only. The subject stays black ink and off-white, so the tint does the identifying.

Fills the circle

Shoulders touch the bottom edge. Floating heads read as clip art at 32px.

Cover the range

Hair types, headwear, coverage. If a new one duplicates an existing silhouette it isn't earning its slot.
