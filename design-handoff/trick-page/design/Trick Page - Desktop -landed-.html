◆ The lowdown

Lift the front wheel and swing it left, then right, hopping the nose across the ground to build speed without pushing.

What you need
Scooter, helmet, and pads once you're on ramps
◆ Tips

Small swings, quick rhythm. Keep your weight over the back wheel the whole time.

Fun fact

It's the first bit of board control most riders learn, and it doubles as a warm-up drill forever after.
