A concrete park in Ventnor with a bowl, ledges, rails, banks and a mini ramp. Ridden by scooter, skate and BMX riders. This page is built from what riders have told us about the spot — the features below are the whole of it.

A street spot in Ventnor: ledges and flat ground, no park features. Ridden by scooter and skate riders. Two features is all this one has, and that is worth knowing before you travel across town for it.
